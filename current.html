Music Today

Music now spreads through streaming playlists and short videos. Here are some of the ways today's music is discovered and shared.

Popular genres

Hip-hop and rap

Still one of the most listened-to genres worldwide.

Pop

Chart-focused music often launched or boosted through social media.

Afrobeats

A genre from West Africa that has grown into a global sound.

K-pop

South Korean pop with a large international fan base.

How social media changes music

Short videos can turn a single song into a hit within days. Artists now often release music with a viral moment in mind, and listeners find new artists through the videos their friends share.

Where to find new music

Check current rankings on sites like Billboard or your favorite streaming app's charts to see what's popular right now.
