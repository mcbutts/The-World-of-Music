How Music Was Recorded and Shared

Each new format changed who could hear music, where, and how. Follow the timeline from early recordings to streaming.

  1. 1877

    The phonograph

    Thomas Edison's machine recorded and played back sound for the first time.

  2. 1948

    The long-playing (LP) record

    Vinyl records introduced by Columbia could hold much more music per side than earlier 78 rpm discs.

  3. 1949

    The 45 rpm single

    RCA Victor introduced a small vinyl record built for one or two hit songs.

  4. 1963

    The cassette tape

    Philips introduced the compact cassette, which let people record and make their own mixtapes.

  5. 1982

    The compact disc (CD)

    CDs went on sale commercially, offering digital sound that did not wear out from play.

  6. 1999

    File sharing and MP3s

    Napster let users share digital music files over the internet, which disrupted how music was sold.

  7. 2008

    Streaming arrives

    Spotify launched, starting the shift from buying music to streaming it on demand.
